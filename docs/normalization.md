# Normalization
An unnormalized row would combine customer, account, account type, transaction, complaint, category, and model fields. 1NF requires atomic fields and keys. 2NF separates customers, accounts, and transactions, removing dependencies on only part of a composite business record. 3NF removes transitive lookup dependencies into `account_types`, `complaint_categories`, and `model_versions`. Each final table is keyed by its identifier; unique email, account number, category name, and username are candidate keys. Non-key attributes depend on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key; the lookup tables are also BCNF.
